Models from my research

Each model plays through its steps. Select a step to read it, or open the original figure.

Triggering Event (Ask)

From action (practice) toward perception (awareness)

  • Identify a problem or gap in understanding.
  • Write a focused question.
  • Enter the question as a prompt to GenAI.

Exploration (Engage)

From perception (awareness) toward deliberation (applicability)

  • Review the AI response.
  • Ask follow-up questions or request clarifications and examples.

Integration (Evaluate)

From deliberation (applicability) toward conception (ideas)

  • Compare GenAI responses with prior knowledge and other credible sources.
  • Check for accuracy, logic, and ethical alignment.

Resolution (Reflect)

From conception (ideas) back to action (practice)

  • Contextualize the GenAI-generated content.
  • Decide how to use the generated content.
  • Apply it to real life.

The Human-in-the-Loop critical inquiry model, built on the Practical Inquiry model of the Community of Inquiry framework. From Nasr et al. (2025), Education Sciences. Read the articleView the original figure

Plan

Set goals and build AI literacy

Identify what you need, set clear goals, and build AI literacy: how AI works, its opportunities, limitations, and responsible use.

Ask

Use the rhetorical situation

Write purposeful, context-specific prompts using elements of the rhetorical situation: audience, genre, purpose, subject, context, exigence, and writer.

Explore

Iterate and refine

Engage in back-and-forth with AI by asking follow-ups, requesting examples, and refining ideas.

Evaluate

Compare and contrast

Examine AI output against what you know, course materials, scholarly sources, and alternative viewpoints.

Reflect

Use the rhetorical triangle for ethical use

Apply the rhetorical triangle (ethos, logos, pathos) to judge credibility, logic, bias, and needed adjustments.

Apply

Use insights authentically

Adapt, integrate, or reject AI-generated content using your own judgment, voice, and disciplinary expectations.

PAEERA: Plan, Ask, Explore, Evaluate, Reflect, and Apply, a six-step instructional model for critically applying generative AI in academic work, from planning and prompt writing to authentic application. Functional AI Literacy (Acquire)

Educator as effective operator

  • Training data and bias
  • Troubleshoot errors
  • Strengths and limitations
  • Customize tools
  • Stay updated

Technical understanding informs ethical judgment.

Ethical AI Literacy (Deepen)

Educator as informed questioner

  • Explore ethics research
  • Co-construct guidelines
  • Analyze ethical issues
  • Follow policies, credit, and disclosure

Ethical judgment guides responsible use.

Rhetorical AI Literacy (Deepen → Create)

Educator as reflective producer

  • Rhetorical situation
  • Prompt strategies
  • Rhetorical triangle and contextual fit
  • Calibrate reliance and authorship

Contextual evaluation informs instructional design.

Pedagogical AI Literacy (Create)

Educator as instructional designer and creator

  • Apply learning theories
  • Integrate for critical thinking, inclusion, and engagement
  • Build activities
  • Adjust methods and assessment
  • Evaluate learning effects
  • Reflect for professional growth

Reflection and professional growth renew the cycle.

Four interrelated literacies organized along UNESCO’s Acquire, Deepen, Create progression and integrated through human-in-the-loop oversight, with the educator as agent, evaluator, and decision-maker. Grounded in UNESCO’s AI Competency Framework for Teachers (Miao & Cukurova, 2024) and Selber’s digital multiliteracies (2004). About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Curriculum and Instruction, with a focus on educational technology, at Northern Arizona University, finishing in May 2027. Before coming to the United States, I prepared future English teachers for more than seven years at Zagazig University in Egypt. I was the first woman from my village to enter higher education and, later, to join the faculty of a College of Education.

My work sits where learning design, AI literacy, and teacher education meet. I design professional learning for teachers, build instruments that measure educators’ AI literacy, and teach online courses in which students feel seen and supported. I am trilingual, in Arabic, English, and French, and I treat the languages and cultures my students bring as assets for everyone’s learning.

Research

Three connected lines of work. Read more on the research page.

Generative AI and critical thinking

How does GenAI shape the way learners question, check, and decide? My systematic review in the Journal of Computers in Education and my Education Sciences study, cited 171 times, examine this question in higher education; you can explore the study’s findings interactively. Through IGNITE-STEM, an NSF-funded project on the Navajo Nation, I am bringing the question to K–12 STEM.

Measuring AI literacy

I developed FERPAI, a scale that measures educators’ functional, ethical, rhetorical, and pedagogical AI literacy. An initial validation with 239 educators is complete; next come separate studies with preservice teachers, in-service teachers, and university faculty.

Designing AI-enhanced professional learning

My dissertation designed and tested the Teachers in the Loop training: microlearning videos, narrated modules, and an avatar-based facilitator. More than 250 teachers, instructional designers, faculty members, and students have completed it.

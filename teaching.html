Teaching

I teach from the student’s side, with high expectations and equally high support.

Students do not come to my courses empty. They bring cultures, languages, work histories, and ways of knowing that can enrich everyone in the room. Following Dewey, I value reflection on experience and the process of learning over any single product. Online, I design for teaching, social, and cognitive presence through online literature circles, culturally grounded portfolios, and personal video feedback, and I use Universal Design for Learning so that every learner has more than one way in. With generative AI, I teach openly rather than around it: students question, explore, check, and decide before they use what a tool produces.

Courses

Contemporary Developments: Generative AI in Education (ETC 599)

Master’s, online, Northern Arizona University, 2024–2026

Four modules that build functional, ethical and rhetorical, and pedagogical GenAI literacy. Students build a GenAI portfolio and finish by designing a human-centered GenAI learning system. Visit the course website.

Technology Planning and Professional Development (ETC 635)

Master’s, online, Northern Arizona University, 2025

Each student carries one GenAI-enhanced course through the full design cycle, from an instructional design proposal to content development, presentation, and peer review.

Foundations of Multicultural Education (BME 210)

Undergraduate, in person and online, Northern Arizona University, 2025–2026

Identity, intersectionality, cultural and economic power, and belonging. Students read Safia Elhillo’s Home Is Not a Country in literature circles and use AI as a thinking partner guided by a critical thinking framework. Visit the course website.

Structured English Immersion (BME 631)

Graduate, online, Northern Arizona University, 2023–2025

Language development, sheltered instruction, and assessment for English learners in K–12 classrooms.

Elementary social studies methods (ECI 407) and supervised practicum (ECI 308/408)

Undergraduate, in person, Northern Arizona University, 2023–2026

Standards-based lesson design and classroom coaching for preservice teachers in K–8 placements.

Teacher preparation and EFL courses

Design and professional learning

I design learning the way I study it: start from what learners need, build it in small pieces they can use right away, and check whether it worked.

Teachers in the Loop

A GenAI literacy training series for educators in STEM, language education, and beyond. I designed and facilitated the live sessions with NAU’s STEM department and AZTESOL, then repackaged them into ten microlearning videos with narrated modules and an avatar-based facilitator, built for asynchronous, low-bandwidth delivery. More than 250 teachers, instructional designers, faculty members, and students in the United States and the Arab world have completed the training.

The live series ran as four sessions, October 29 to November 19, 2025:

  1. Identifying what generative AI is, its possibilities, and its limitations
  2. Applying critical thinking and effective prompt engineering for ethical AI use
  3. Theory-informed teaching and learning for effective, balanced human–AI classrooms
  4. Teaching effectively in AI-enhanced classrooms: implementation, student guidance, inclusive support, and authentic assessment

Course design

ETC 599, Contemporary Developments. A four-module sequence that mirrors my AI literacy framework, moving in order from functional literacy to ethical and rhetorical literacy, then to two modules of pedagogical literacy. Visit the course website.

ETC 635, Technology Planning and Professional Development. A project-based course in which each student designs one GenAI-enhanced course through ideation, planning, content development, finalization, and peer review.

BME 210, Foundations of Multicultural Education. A course site organized around identity, belonging, and critical AI use. Visit the course website.

Generative Artificial Intelligence and Data Analysis. A new online course I designed for NAU’s Educational Technology program.

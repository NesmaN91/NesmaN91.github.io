## Adding pictures

### How photo spaces work

Every page has spaces waiting for photos. A space stays invisible until you upload a picture with its exact file name, and then the photo appears automatically. Visitors never see an empty box.

**To see every empty space and its file name,** add `?slots` to the end of any page address, for example:

- `https://nesman91.github.io/highlights.html?slots`
- `https://nesman91.github.io/index.html?slots`

Dashed boxes show the file name each space expects.

### Upload a photo

1. **Check permissions.** Post photos only with permission from the people in them. For K–12 students and Tuba City community members, use photos only with written consent and in line with the project's community and IRB agreements.
2. **Prepare the photo.** Use a JPG about 1600 pixels wide and under about 500 KB. The free tool https://squoosh.app resizes and compresses photos in your browser.
3. **Rename it** to the exact name from the checklist below, in lowercase.
4. **Upload it.** In this repository, open the right folder (for example `images/highlights`), click **Add file**, then **Upload files**, drag the photo in, and click **Commit changes**.
5. **Check the site.** Reload the page after a minute. If the photo does not show, force a fresh load with **Ctrl + F5** on Windows or **Cmd + Shift + R** on a Mac.

**Your profile photo** is `images/profile.jpg`. Use a portrait shape, about 800 × 1000 pixels. Until you upload it, your initials show instead.

**The home carousel** shows the first photo of each main highlight (the files ending in `-1.jpg` listed under "Home carousel"). It appears by itself once at least one of those photos is uploaded, and it moves on its own once there are two or more.

### Change a caption or alt text

1. Open the page's `.html` file and click the **pencil icon**.
2. Find the photo's file name. Change the words in `alt="..."` (a description for screen readers) and, if there is one, the text between `<figcaption>` and `</figcaption>`.
3. Click **Commit changes**.

### Add a new highlight, such as a new conference

1. Open `highlights.html` and click the **pencil icon**.
2. Copy an existing block from `<article class="moment"` down to its matching `</article>`, and paste it where you want the new highlight.
3. Change the `id`, the photo file names, the date, the title, the text, and the links.
4. Upload the photos with the new file names.

### Add a link

Inside any text, paste:

```html
<a href="https://the-address.org">the words people click</a>
```

Each highlight has a line marked `<!-- To add a link -->` that shows where its links go.

## The three animated models

The home page opens with your three models, behind tabs:

- the Human-in-the-Loop inquiry model (*Education Sciences*, 2025)
- the PAEERA model (*Journal of Computers in Education*, 2026)
- the FERPAI framework

The Research page shows each model inside its research line. Each model plays through its steps; visitors can click any step, or a part of the diagram, to read it. Hovering over a model pauses it.

- The wording for each step is in `index.html` and `research.html`. To change a step's text, edit it inside the matching `<article role="tabpanel">` block, in both files.
- The original figures are in `images/frameworks/`, and visitors can open them with "View the original figure." To replace a figure, upload a new picture with the same file name.

## Motion

The site includes:

- a gold dot that travels around the Ask, Explore, Check, Decide loop, with each step glowing as the dot passes
- numbers that count up the first time they scroll into view
- sections that ease in as you scroll
- a photo carousel that moves on its own and pauses when you hover over it or touch it
- photos that open large when clicked
- filter buttons with a short fade

Visitors who have turned on "reduce motion" on their device see the same content without the animation. To slow the loop or change its speed, edit the `12s` values in `css/style.css` and in the `<animateMotion>` line of `index.html`.

## Editing text

1. Open any `.html` file and click the **pencil icon**.
2. Change only the words between the tags. Leave anything inside `< >` alone.
3. Click **Commit changes**.

**Edit several files at once.** On this repository's page, press the **.** (period) key. GitHub opens a full editor in your browser.

To add a news item, paste this at the top of the News list in `index.html`:

```html
<li><time datetime="2027-05">May 2027</time><span>Defended my dissertation at Northern Arizona University.</span></li>
```

To update the citation count, search the pages for `171` and change the number.

## Use your own domain (optional)

1. Buy a domain, for example `nesmanasr.com`, from a registrar such as Cloudflare, Porkbun, or Namecheap. A `.com` usually costs about $10 to $20 a year.
2. In this repository, go to **Settings**, then **Pages**, then **Custom domain**, type the domain, and click **Save**.
3. At your registrar, add four **A records** for the main domain pointing to `185.199.108.153`, `185.199.109.153`, `185.199.110.153`, and `185.199.111.153`. Add a **CNAME** record for `www` pointing to `nesman91.github.io`.
4. Once the check turns green, tick **Enforce HTTPS**.
